# DESIGN.md

Author: product owner (transcribed from the Game Plan quiz rework brief).

## Product

AI Powered Game Plan Quiz. Cold traffic to a booked discovery call. First-person voice as Ross. Twenty numbered questions, one entry fork, four beats, email capture, emailed Game Plan.

## Audience

Founders, operators, consultants, and people who lead a team. They want a plan, not a lecture.

## Personality

Fun, but professional and premium. Quick like drawing a card, not filling a form. Honest. No fake reviews, no invented stats.

## Visual language

- Palette: existing AI Powered tokens. Ink `#2d2d2d`, muted accent `#7a8fa6`, rules `#c8d0d8`, grey copy `#8a8a8a` / `#464646`. Light default. Working light/dark toggle.
- Type: DM Sans. Headlines tight tracking, body 16px, readable.
- Motif: a deck of offset stacked cards. Peek of the next card below the one in play. Answering sends the card out to the left. Results swipe to the back of the stack.
- Max width: 720px.
- Radius: cards generous, options tighter, badges squared. Not every control is a pill.
- Icons: only on concrete choices (who you are, tools). No icon on abstract answers.
- Motion: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2. Card advance and result flip have a job. Hover is not enough; full-page bounce is too much.

## Dial

ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2

## Content rules

- Copy is first person, as Ross, from the Game Plan funnel.
- Proof headcount stays `[REAL DATA]` until confirmed.
- Self-paced finish-rate percentages are omitted until confirmed. Keep the point: a room, a deadline, and someone noticing.
- Capture bonus is the emailed Game Plan / share link. No extra gift.
- Discovery CTA: https://aipowered.xyz/contact

## Why these choices

- Stacked cards: the quiz should feel like a short hand of cards, not a Typeform. The cool grey page wash is the table the deck sits on, from the muted accent `#7a8fa6`, not a purple glow.
- Current colours: keep the brand, do not borrow the artifact beige or Coursiv purple.
- 720px: a bit tighter than 840 so the quiz reads as a card in the hand, not a wide page.
- Track A/B/C: every path ends on a programme, not a generic score out of 100.
